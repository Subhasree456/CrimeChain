## 📌 Project Overview

CrimeChain is an AI-powered blockchain-based digital evidence management system designed to securely store, manage, verify, and track digital forensic evidence.

The platform combines blockchain technology, cryptographic hashing, AI-powered analysis, and digital audit trails to help maintain the integrity and traceability of digital evidence.

It aims to address challenges associated with evidence tampering, unauthorized modifications, and the lack of transparent evidence tracking.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Digital evidence plays a crucial role in modern cybercrime investigations. However, maintaining its authenticity and integrity throughout the investigation process remains a challenge.

Traditional evidence management systems may lack transparent tracking mechanisms and reliable tamper-detection capabilities.

CrimeChain addresses these challenges through cryptographic hashing, blockchain-based evidence registration, integrity verification, and audit tracking.

---

## 💡 Key Features

- 🔐 **Secure Evidence Management** – Upload and manage digital forensic evidence.
- ⛓️ **Blockchain Registration** – Register evidence records with associated blockchain hashes.
- 🔑 **SHA-256 Hashing** – Generate cryptographic fingerprints for digital evidence.
- 🛡️ **Evidence Verification** – Detect modifications by comparing cryptographic hashes.
- 🤖 **AI-Powered Analysis** – Analyse digital evidence and generate insights.
- 📜 **Audit Trail** – Maintain a record of evidence-related activities.
- 🔍 **Blockchain Explorer** – View registered blocks and transaction information.
- 📊 **Dashboard** – Monitor evidence statistics and system activity.

## 🔄 System Workflow

1. Investigator uploads digital evidence.
2. System generates a SHA-256 hash.
3. Evidence information and hash are registered in the blockchain ledger.
4. AI analysis processes the available evidence.
5. Investigators can verify evidence integrity.
6. Any modification to the original file can be detected through hash comparison.
7. Evidence-related activities are recorded in the audit trail.
